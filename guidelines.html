Proper Look-out

Guidelines

Last updated 15 November 2026

A short list of what is welcome and what is not, so that the material stays worth paying for.

Please do

• Use Proper Look-out for your own study, on your own device.

• Keep your account, password and licence key private.

• Back up your progress, with a backup code or by signing in.

• Check anything important against the official publications in force.

• Tell the seller about a mistake in the content. Corrections are welcome.

Please do not

• Share your account, plan or key, or pass it from person to person.

• Copy, photograph, screenshot, record, print, extract, publish or sell the course material.

• Try to unlock paid content without paying, or use a modified copy of the app.

• Alter, decompile or reverse-engineer the product, or interfere with its checks.

• Use scripts or tools to collect the content automatically.

• Attack the online service or other people’s accounts, or use the product to spread malware.

What happens if the guidelines are broken

First the product simply refuses: copying, printing and screenshots are switched off. If the attempts are repeated, you see a warning that the account may be blocked, and the seller is told. If they continue, the account or key may be blocked.

A single accidental tap, a keyboard shortcut pressed out of habit, or a fault in the product is not treated as misuse. If you get a warning you think is wrong, contact the seller.
