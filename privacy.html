Proper Look-out

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy says what Proper Look-out records, why, where it is kept and how you can have it deleted. It covers the Android app and the website.

Who is responsible

Proper Look-out is sold and run by its developer (“the seller”). For any question about your data, or to have it deleted, write to: the contact details you were given with your licence key, or the developer contact e-mail shown on the Proper Look-out page in the Google Play Store.

What stays on your device

Your study progress, your settings, your reminders and the state of your plan or licence key are stored on your own device. If you never create an account, none of this is sent to the seller.

Reminders and other notifications are made on your phone by the app itself. Nothing is sent anywhere to show them.

If you create an account

Creating an account is optional. If you do, the following is kept online so that you can sign in, back up your progress and move to another device:

• your e-mail address and a protected form of your password. Sign-in is handled by Google’s Firebase Authentication; the seller cannot see your password;

• your cloud backup: study progress, settings and, on the website, your licence key;

• a device code (a random-looking code made by the product, not your phone number or IMEI) and the time you last used the product.

Activity and security records

To protect the course material and your account, the product keeps a short record of activity on each account, or on each licence key if you have no account:

• when you sign in and out, and which device code is in use;

• which sections you open and on which days, as simple counts. Your answers to questions are not included, except inside your own backup;

• security events: repeated attempts to copy, print, save or capture the content; many wrong keys or passwords in a short time; signs that the product has been altered; and the same account moving between many devices.

A single accidental tap or keyboard shortcut is ignored. Only repeated attempts are recorded. If they continue you are shown a warning, the seller is told, and the account may be blocked.

These records are seen only by the seller. They are not used for advertising and are never sold.

Online games

Playing the CPU needs no account and sends nothing. Playing other people online needs an account. Then the following is kept online, with Google Firebase:

• the game name and colour you choose, and a friend code made by the product. Other signed-in players can see your game name, colour and whether you are online; your e-mail address is never shown to them;

• your friends list, friend requests and game invitations, and the players you have blocked. Only you can read your own lists;

• for each match: the room, the players in it and each answer with the time it reached the server, so that every device shows the same score; and your game records (games played, games won, best score).

A blocked player cannot send you friend requests or invitations. Deleting your account removes your game name, friend code, friends, requests, invitations and records.

One device at a time

A plan, key or account works on one device at a time. To make that work, the product stores the device code that currently holds it and the time it was claimed.

Payments

In the Android app, plans are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the seller does not receive your card or UPI details.

A licence key for the website is bought directly from the seller. The seller then receives what you send with your payment: your name, the payment reference and the contact you used.

What Proper Look-out does not do

No advertising. No sale or sharing of your data for marketing. No tracking across other apps or websites. The product does not read your contacts, location, photos, microphone or files.

Blocking of screenshots, copying and printing happens on your device and collects nothing by itself.

Where it is kept and for how long

Account data, backups and activity records are stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), protected in transit by encryption. They are kept for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from the Account page (“Delete my account”); this removes your sign-in, your backup and your activity record. You can also ask the seller to do it using the contact details above. Records of confirmed misuse may be kept for up to 12 months so that a blocked person cannot simply return.

Younger users

Proper Look-out is made for maritime students and officers. If you are under 18, create an account only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is shown here with its date. A change that affects what is recorded about you is also announced inside the product.
